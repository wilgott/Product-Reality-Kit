# Example Company: VerifyFlow

This is a fictional example.

Do not use real customer names, real revenue numbers, or private company data in public examples.

---

## Company

VerifyFlow is a B2B SaaS company that helps regulated companies build customer onboarding and verification workflows.

The product is used by compliance, operations, and growth teams.

Customers can create flows without asking Engineering to build everything from scratch.

---

## Product

VerifyFlow includes:

- Identity verification steps
- Document collection
- Risk checks
- Manual review queues
- Workflow rules
- Admin configuration
- Reporting
- Integrations with third-party providers

---

## Customer segments

VerifyFlow sells to:

- SMBs with simple onboarding needs
- Mid-market companies with more complex workflows
- Enterprise companies with compliance, audit, and approval requirements

The company is sales-led.

Sales works closely with Product, CS, and Engineering because customer needs can be complex.

---

## Current situation

VerifyFlow is moving upmarket.

That creates pressure.

SMB customers want speed and simplicity.

Mid-market customers want flexibility.

Enterprise customers want control, auditability, permissions, and exceptions.

Sales wants to close larger deals.

Engineering wants to keep the workflow engine maintainable.

CS wants onboarding to stay understandable.

Product needs to make the call.

---

## Product principles for this example

1. Start with the customer problem.
2. Prefer reusable patterns over one-off custom work.
3. Do not add configurability before the problem is understood.
4. Enterprise requests should be evaluated, not blindly accepted.
5. A yes to one thing is a no to something else.
6. Decisions should be written down so the team remembers why they were made.
