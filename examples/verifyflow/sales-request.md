# Example: Sales Request

This is a fictional example.

---

## Situation

A large enterprise prospect is evaluating VerifyFlow.

The prospect says they will sign a EUR 180k ARR contract if VerifyFlow builds custom approval logic for their compliance team.

Sales wants Product to commit the feature this quarter.

Engineering says the request touches the workflow engine and may add long-term complexity.

CS says some existing customers have asked for more approval flexibility, but not in the exact same way.

Product needs to decide if this is:

- A strategic product capability
- A one-off enterprise exception
- A pricing or packaging opportunity
- Implementation work
- Something to say no to for now

---

## What Sales says

> This customer is important. They are ready to sign, but they need custom approval logic. They also said other vendors can support more flexible approvals. If we can commit this quarter, we have a strong chance of winning the deal.

---

## What the customer says

The customer wants to create different approval paths depending on:

- Customer risk level
- Country
- Product type
- Document type
- Manual review result
- Compliance team availability

They want some flows to require one approval and other flows to require two approvals.

They also want a senior compliance user to override the flow in special cases.

---

## What Engineering says

Engineering says this is not just a UI change.

It affects:

- Workflow rules
- Permissions
- Audit logs
- Manual review queues
- Testing combinations
- Customer support
- Future workflow changes

Engineering thinks a one-off solution would make the workflow engine harder to maintain.

---

## What CS says

CS says the problem is real, but not yet clearly defined.

Some customers have asked for:

- Better approval control
- More audit visibility
- Easier manual review handover
- Fewer workarounds in compliance workflows

But most customers have not asked for this exact approval logic.

---

## Current evidence

What we know:

- One large enterprise prospect is asking for this exact solution.
- Three existing mid-market customers have mentioned approval flexibility.
- Two lost deals mentioned "compliance workflow control" in notes.
- No clear usage data exists because the current product has limited approval configuration.
- Engineering expects medium to high complexity.
- Sales sees high short-term revenue potential.

What we do not know:

- Whether this is needed by the broader ICP.
- Whether the requested solution is the right solution.
- Whether customers would pay extra for advanced approval control.
- Whether this should be product, add-on, implementation, or service.
- Whether a simpler workflow template would solve most of the problem.

---

## Pressure

Revenue pressure is real.

But the product risk is also real.

This should not be decided only by the size of the deal.

It should be decided by whether the customer problem is important, reusable, and aligned with the market VerifyFlow wants to serve.
