# AI Prompt: Review a Product Decision

Use this when you have a draft Product Decision Record and want to pressure-test it before sharing.

The goal is not to let AI approve the decision.

The goal is to find weak logic, missing evidence, unclear trade-offs, and communication risks.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me review a B2B SaaS Product Decision Record.

Be direct, but fair.
Do not rewrite everything unless needed.
Help me improve the decision quality.

Here is the Product Decision Record:

[Paste PDR here]

Please review it and return:

1. The decision in one clear sentence
2. Whether the customer problem is clear
3. Whether the evidence is strong, weak, or missing
4. The biggest assumption in the decision
5. The trade-off we are accepting
6. What we are saying no to
7. What could go wrong with this decision
8. What would make us reverse the decision
9. Who may disagree with the decision and why
10. What Sales, CS, Engineering, Support, Marketing, or leadership need to understand
11. What part of the decision should be written more clearly
12. A stronger version of the decision summary
13. Three questions I should answer before sharing this

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
What part of this decision would be misunderstood by Sales, CS, or Engineering?
```

```text
Where am I hiding uncertainty behind confident language?
```

```text
What would future-us wish we had written down?
```
