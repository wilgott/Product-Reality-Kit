# AI Prompt: Diagnose Feature Adoption

Use this when a shipped feature is not being used as much as expected.

The goal is not to decide the feature is bad.

The goal is to understand whether the right customers know about it, can find it, understand it, and need it in their real workflow.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me diagnose a B2B SaaS feature adoption problem.

Do not assume low usage means the feature failed.
Do not assume customers know the feature exists.
Do not jump straight to UI changes.
Do not invent evidence.

Separate known facts from unknowns. If adoption expectations were never defined, say so.

Help me understand whether low adoption is caused by weak awareness, poor discovery, unclear value, wrong target user, wrong package, setup friction, workflow mismatch, Sales or CS enablement gaps, or a product quality problem.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product:
- Feature:
- Target customer:
- Target user:
- Customer problem the feature should solve:
- Expected adoption:
- Actual adoption:
- Usage by segment:
- Usage by plan or package:
- Launch or communication history:
- In-product discovery:
- Setup needed before value:
- Sales feedback:
- CS feedback:
- Support feedback:
- Customer feedback:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. The likely customer problem behind low adoption
2. What we know from evidence
3. What is still unknown
4. Whether low adoption is actually a problem for this feature
5. Common false diagnoses and red flags
6. Whether this looks like awareness, discovery, value clarity, workflow fit, packaging, setup, enablement, or product quality
7. Questions to ask customers who used it
8. Questions to ask customers who did not use it
9. Questions to ask Sales, CS, Support, Marketing, and Engineering
10. Decision options and trade-offs
11. Evidence gaps to close before changing the product
12. What not to change yet
13. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
What is the weakest evidence in our adoption diagnosis?
```

```text
Are we measuring adoption among the customers who actually should use this feature?
```

```text
What would we do first if the issue is awareness, not product quality?
```
