# AI Prompt: Pressure-Test Pricing and Packaging

Use this when pricing or packaging feels confusing.

The goal is not to let AI set your price.

The goal is to understand what is actually confusing.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me pressure-test a B2B SaaS pricing and packaging problem.

Do not jump straight to changing prices.
Do not assume cheaper is better.
Do not assume the problem is only pricing.

Help me understand if this is a pricing problem, packaging problem, positioning problem, onboarding problem, sales enablement problem, or ICP problem.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product:
- Customer segment:
- Sales motion:
- Current pricing model:
- Current packages or plans:
- What customers are confused about:
- What Sales is struggling with:
- What CS is seeing:
- Discount patterns:
- Expansion challenges:
- Churn signals:
- Competitor context:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. What the real confusion might be
2. Whether this looks like pricing, packaging, positioning, onboarding, sales enablement, or ICP
3. What customers likely think they are buying
4. What value might be unclear
5. What Sales may be forced to explain too much
6. What package or tier problems may exist
7. Red flags in the current setup
8. Questions to ask customers
9. Questions to ask Sales and CS
10. Data to check before changing anything
11. Decision options and trade-offs
12. What not to change yet
13. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
What is the most likely false diagnosis in this pricing discussion?
```

```text
Are we packaging around customer value or around our internal product structure?
```

```text
What would be the simplest change that could reduce confusion without changing the whole pricing model?
```
