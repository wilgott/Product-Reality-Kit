# AI Prompt: Diagnose a Sales Request

Use this when Sales, leadership, or a large customer is pushing for a feature.

The goal is not to make AI decide.

The goal is to make the thinking clearer.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me diagnose a B2B SaaS sales request.

Do not jump to a solution.
Do not assume Sales is wrong.
Do not assume the customer is right.

Help me understand the customer problem, the business pressure, the trade-offs, and the decision options.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product:
- Customer segment:
- Sales motion:
- Request:
- Who is asking:
- Revenue impact:
- Timeline pressure:
- Current roadmap impact:
- What Sales says:
- What CS says:
- What Engineering says:
- What customers have said before:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. The likely customer problem behind the request
2. What might be a real market signal
3. What might be a one-off request
4. What might actually be a pricing, packaging, GTM, onboarding, or sales enablement issue
5. Red flags I should not ignore
6. Questions I should ask Sales
7. Questions I should ask the customer
8. Questions I should ask CS and Support
9. Questions I should ask Engineering
10. Decision options: build now, discover more, workaround, paid add-on, defer, say no
11. The trade-off for each option
12. What evidence would make the decision stronger
13. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## How to use the output

Do not copy the answer blindly.

Use it to:

- Find weak assumptions
- Prepare better questions
- Write a clearer Product Decision Record
- Explain the trade-off to Sales, CS, Engineering, and leadership

---

## Good follow-up questions

After the first answer, ask:

```text
What is the weakest part of our current thinking?
```

```text
What would make this a bad product decision six months from now?
```

```text
What would we need to believe for this request to deserve roadmap capacity?
```
