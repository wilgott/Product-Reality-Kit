# AI Prompt: Diagnose Launch

Use this when a product or feature shipped, but customers, prospects, Sales, or CS did not respond as expected.

The goal is not to blame Marketing.

The goal is to find the weak point in the launch chain.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me diagnose a B2B SaaS launch that did not land.

Do not assume Marketing failed.
Do not assume the product is bad.
Do not assume we only need more promotion.
Do not invent evidence.

Separate known facts from unknowns. If launch success was never defined, say so.

Help me understand whether the launch problem is product, positioning, target segment, value clarity, packaging, pricing, Sales enablement, CS readiness, in-product discovery, timing, proof, or customer understanding.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product or feature:
- Target customer:
- Target user:
- Sales motion:
- What launched:
- Customer problem it should solve:
- Launch date:
- Expected launch outcome:
- Actual launch outcome:
- Launch channels:
- Marketing message:
- Sales material:
- CS readiness:
- In-product discovery:
- Visitor, lead, demo, or usage data:
- Customer feedback:
- Sales feedback:
- CS feedback:
- Support feedback:
- Packaging or pricing context:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. The likely customer problem behind the weak launch response
2. What we know from evidence
3. What is still unknown
4. The weakest point in the launch chain
5. Common false diagnoses and red flags
6. Whether this looks like product, positioning, GTM, Sales, CS, segment, timing, packaging, or a mix
7. Questions to ask customers and prospects
8. Questions to ask Marketing, Sales, CS, Support, and Engineering
9. Decision options and trade-offs
10. Evidence gaps to close before relaunching or building more
11. What not to do yet
12. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
Did we launch a customer outcome, or only announce shipped work?
```

```text
What is the most likely false diagnosis in this launch review?
```

```text
What would we change if the product is usable but the value story is unclear?
```
