# AI Prompt: Diagnose Activation

Use this when customers buy, sign up, or start onboarding but do not reach first value.

The goal is not to blame the customer.

The goal is to understand where the path from signed customer to real value is breaking.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me diagnose a B2B SaaS activation problem.

Do not jump to onboarding emails.
Do not assume the customer is lazy.
Do not assume the product is broken.
Do not invent evidence.

Separate what we know from what we do not know. If evidence is weak, say so plainly.

Help me understand whether weak activation is caused by product setup, onboarding, Sales expectations, implementation, customer readiness, buyer/user mismatch, integrations, data, approvals, or unclear first value.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product:
- Customer segment:
- Sales motion:
- What first value should mean:
- Current activation definition:
- Expected time to first value:
- Actual time to first value:
- Where customers stop:
- Required setup steps:
- Customer data needed:
- Integrations or approvals needed:
- Sales promises:
- CS/onboarding feedback:
- Support feedback:
- Customer feedback:
- Usage data:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. The likely customer problem behind weak activation
2. What we know from evidence
3. What is still unknown
4. The most likely activation bottleneck
5. Common false diagnoses and red flags
6. Whether this looks like product, onboarding, Sales, implementation, customer readiness, or a mix
7. Questions to ask customers
8. Questions to ask Sales, CS, Support, onboarding, and Engineering
9. Decision options and trade-offs
10. What evidence would make the decision stronger
11. What not to change yet
12. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
What is the weakest assumption in our activation diagnosis?
```

```text
Where are we blaming customer engagement when we may have created a hard path to value?
```

```text
What would we change first if we had to reduce time to first value without adding more manual help?
```
