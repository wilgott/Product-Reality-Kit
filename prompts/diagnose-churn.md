# AI Prompt: Diagnose Churn

Use this when customers are leaving and the team is moving too quickly toward feature work.

The goal is not to assign blame.

The goal is to separate product problems from Sales, onboarding, implementation, ICP, pricing, support, dependency, and value problems.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me diagnose a B2B SaaS churn problem.

Do not assume churn means the product failed.
Do not assume missing features are the cause.
Do not assume CS should have saved the account.
Do not invent evidence.

Separate known facts from unknowns. If the churn reason is vague, call that out.

Help me understand whether churn is caused by product gaps, weak activation, bad fit, unclear Sales promises, hard implementation, low usage, poor support, pricing, budget, buyer/user mismatch, external dependencies, or value that was not visible before renewal.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product:
- Customer segment:
- Customer cohort:
- Package or plan:
- Sales motion:
- Churn type:
- Churn timing:
- Stated churn reason:
- Usage before churn:
- Activation status:
- First value reached:
- Sales promises:
- Onboarding or implementation notes:
- CS notes:
- Support history:
- Renewal notes:
- Competitor or pricing context:
- External dependencies:
- Customer fit:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. The likely customer problem behind the churn
2. What we know from evidence
3. What is still unknown
4. Whether this looks like product, Sales, onboarding, implementation, ICP, pricing, support, dependency, budget, or a mix
5. Common false diagnoses and red flags
6. Questions to ask churned customers
7. Questions to ask Sales, CS, Support, onboarding, and Engineering
8. Patterns to check by segment, cohort, package, use case, and sales channel
9. Decision options and trade-offs
10. Evidence gaps that should be closed before roadmap changes
11. What not to blame too quickly
12. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
What is the most likely false diagnosis in this churn discussion?
```

```text
Where does the evidence point to a delivery or fit problem instead of a product gap?
```

```text
What would we need to learn before turning this churn pattern into roadmap work?
```
