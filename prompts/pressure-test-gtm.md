# AI Prompt: Pressure-Test GTM

Use this when a launch, feature, or product is not landing.

The goal is not to blame Marketing, Sales, CS, or Product.

The goal is to understand where the go-to-market chain is weak.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me pressure-test a B2B SaaS go-to-market problem.

Do not assume the product is bad.
Do not assume the GTM team failed.
Do not assume we only need more marketing.

Help me understand if the problem is product, positioning, packaging, onboarding, sales enablement, customer segment, timing, or value clarity.

Context:
- Company type:
- Product or feature:
- Customer segment:
- Sales motion:
- What we launched:
- Who it was for:
- Customer problem it should solve:
- Launch date:
- Expected outcome:
- Actual outcome:
- Adoption data:
- Sales feedback:
- CS feedback:
- Support feedback:
- Marketing activity:
- Customer feedback:
- Packaging or pricing context:
- Data we have:
- Data we do not have:

Please return:

1. What may have failed: product, positioning, packaging, onboarding, enablement, segment, timing, or value clarity
2. The most likely false diagnosis
3. Whether the customer problem is clear enough
4. Whether the target customer is specific enough
5. Whether Sales can explain the value in one minute
6. Whether CS can onboard it without heroics
7. Whether customers can reach value quickly
8. What questions to ask customers
9. What questions to ask Sales, CS, Support, and Marketing
10. What data to check before changing the product
11. Decision options and trade-offs
12. What not to do yet
13. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
Did we launch a feature, or did we launch a change in customer behavior?
```

```text
What would need to be true for this to be a product problem?
```

```text
What would need to be true for this to be a GTM problem?
```
