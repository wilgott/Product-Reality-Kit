# AI Prompt: Challenge an Enterprise Request

Use this when a large customer asks for something that may pull the roadmap sideways.

The goal is not to dismiss the customer.

The goal is to understand if the request is a real market signal or custom work in disguise.

---

## Prompt

Copy and paste this into your AI tool.

```text
You are helping me evaluate a B2B SaaS enterprise customer request.

Do not assume the customer is wrong.
Do not assume the request is strategic.
Do not jump to a yes or no.

Help me understand if this is:
- a reusable product capability
- a one-off customer exception
- a compliance or procurement checkbox
- a workflow problem
- a paid add-on opportunity
- implementation/service work
- something we should say no to

Context:
- Company type:
- Product:
- Customer segment:
- Customer size:
- Request:
- Why the customer wants it:
- Revenue or renewal impact:
- Timeline pressure:
- Similar requests we have seen:
- Current workaround:
- Product area affected:
- Engineering concerns:
- CS/support concerns:
- Sales view:
- Strategic context:
- What we do not know:

Please return:

1. The customer problem behind the request
2. Whether this looks like a one-off or a market pattern
3. What evidence supports building it
4. What evidence supports not building it
5. Risks if we build it exactly as requested
6. Risks if we say no
7. A simpler or more reusable version of the solution
8. Whether this should be core product, paid add-on, service, workaround, or no
9. Questions to ask the customer
10. Questions to ask Sales, CS, Support, and Engineering
11. Decision options and trade-offs
12. Reversal criteria
13. A draft Product Decision Record in plain English

Use clear language.
Be direct.
Avoid consultant-speak.
```

---

## Good follow-up questions

```text
What would make this request a bad product decision one year from now?
```

```text
How could we solve the broader problem without building the exact thing the customer asked for?
```

```text
What evidence would prove this is useful for more than one customer?
```
