# Influences and References

Product Reality Kit is not trying to invent product management from scratch.

It is a practical toolkit shaped by B2B SaaS product practice, public product management resources, and decision-record patterns.

## Grounding model

The toolkit separates three kinds of material:

- **Source-backed:** Public sources support the idea directly. These are listed below.
- **Experience-backed:** Practical B2B SaaS judgment from sales-led, enterprise-heavy, workflow, no-code, orchestration, SMB, mid-market, and enterprise product environments. The diagnostics fall mostly into this category.
- **Project philosophy:** The toolkit's point of view, such as starting with the customer problem, understanding business pressure, making the trade-off clear, and then deciding.

If a broad claim cannot be sourced, the toolkit should frame it as a question, a risk, a pattern to check, or an opinionated product judgment.

The current version does not make academic, book-based, or formal research-backed claims. Books and articles listed as candidates are there for future review, not as citations for the current diagnostics.

## Content grounding by section

| Section | Why it exists | Grounding |
|---|---|---|
| README | Explain the toolkit, audience, and product point of view. | Project philosophy and B2B SaaS product experience. |
| Diagnostics | Help PMs separate the visible request from the underlying customer problem, business pressure, options, and trade-offs. | Experience-backed product judgment. The content is written as questions, red flags, and decision options rather than universal claims. |
| Product Decision Records | Help teams record what was decided, why, what trade-off was accepted, and when to revisit it. | Source-backed by ADR patterns, adapted for product decisions. |

## Decision records

Product Decision Records are inspired by Architecture Decision Records.

ADRs are short documents that capture a decision, the context for making it, and the consequences. Product Reality Kit adapts that idea for product decisions: customer problem, business pressure, options, trade-offs, reversal criteria, and communication.

Source:

- Martin Fowler, [Architecture Decision Record](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ArchitectureDecisionRecord.html)

## Product documentation

Product Reality Kit is designed to work with the places teams already write product decisions: Confluence, Notion, Jira, Linear, GitHub, Google Docs, or a plain Markdown repo.

The goal is not to replace PRDs or product documentation tools. It is to make the decision behind the work easier to explain and remember.

Source:

- Atlassian, [Product requirements document template](https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence/templates/product-requirements)

## Public product management resources

The public GitHub product-management space already includes open-source product tools, PM skill libraries, prompt collections, templates, and curated resource lists.

Product Reality Kit takes a narrower path: practical B2B SaaS diagnostics and product decision records.

Sources:

- GitHub, [product-management topic](https://github.com/topics/product-management)
- Dean Peters, [product-manager-prompts](https://github.com/deanpeters/product-manager-prompts)
